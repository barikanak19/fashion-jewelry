A PRACTICAL GUIDE FOR SMALL BUSINESSES

How to Use This Guide

Explore these 10 chapters to learn how to present, promote and improve a small business online. The examples are illustrative; adapt them to your own business.

Chapter 1: Introduction to Digital Business

What is a digital business?

A digital business uses the internet and digital tools to promote, display and sell products, and to connect with customers. A small business owner can start online without opening a physical shop in a new location.

Example

A jewellery seller working from home can display earrings, prices and product photos on a website so customers can explore them online.

Why is digital business important?

  • It helps more people discover your business.
  • Customers can view products without visiting your shop.
  • You can share product details and prices online.
  • You can communicate through WhatsApp or social media.
  • You can promote your business with a small budget.

What do you need to get started?

  • A smartphone or computer and an internet connection.
  • Product or service photos, names, prices and descriptions.
  • A way for customers to contact you or place orders.
Activity

Think about your business: What do you sell? Who are your customers? Why should they choose your business?

Chapter 3: Creating Your Business Profile

A business profile introduces your business to potential customers. It helps them understand what you sell, where you operate and how to contact you.

Important elements of a business profile

  1. Business name: The name customers use to identify your business.
  2. Business logo: A symbol or design representing your business.
  3. Business description: A short explanation of your products or services.
  4. Business category: Your type of business, such as jewellery, clothing, food or beauty services.
  5. Contact information: Your business phone number, email or WhatsApp contact.
  6. Location: Your shop address or service area, if relevant.
  7. Business hours: The days and times customers can contact or visit you.
Illustrative jewellery business profile

Shine Jewellery · Fashion Jewellery · Affordable, stylish jewellery for everyday wear and special occasions · Palghar, Maharashtra · Business WhatsApp number · Monday to Saturday, 10 AM–7 PM.

Tips for a good profile

  • Use a clear, professional business name and simple language.
  • Keep contact details correct and use your own business photos and logo.
  • Avoid sharing private personal information unnecessarily.
Activity

Write a short profile for your business using the details above.

Chapter 4: Product Photography and Product Descriptions

Customers cannot physically touch a product online. Good photographs and clear descriptions help them understand what they are buying.

How to take good product photographs

  • Clean the product and use natural or suitable lighting.
  • Choose a clean background and keep the product in focus.
  • Take photos from different angles.
  • Avoid excessive filters that change the product's appearance.
  • Use your own photographs whenever possible.

What to include in a product description

  • What the product is and what material it is made from.
  • Its colour, size and price.
  • How customers can use or style it.
  • Whether it is in stock.
Example

Pearl Beaded Bracelet
A pearl-style beaded bracelet for everyday wear and casual occasions.
Material: Specify the actual material. Price: ₹299. Availability: In stock, if confirmed. Care: Keep away from water and perfume if recommended for the actual product.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Blurry images, misleading descriptions or incorrect prices.
  • Claiming an item is gold or silver without verifying its material.
  • Showing unavailable products as available.
Activity

Choose one product and prepare a photograph, product name, price and two-line description.

Chapter 5: Introduction to Digital Marketing

What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing means promoting products or services using the internet and digital platforms. A small business can start with free tools and gradually try paid advertising if needed.

Main types of digital marketing

  1. Social media marketing: Promote through posts, stories, videos and reels on platforms such as Instagram and Facebook.
  2. WhatsApp marketing: Share catalogues, updates and offers with customers who have agreed to receive messages.
  3. Content marketing: Create useful content such as styling tips, product-care advice and gift ideas.
  4. Search engine optimization (SEO): Improve website content so search engines can understand the business and may show it in relevant results.
  5. Email marketing: Send updates, offers or newsletters to customers who have opted in.
  6. Paid advertising: Pay platforms to display advertisements to selected audiences.
Example

A jewellery seller can post clear photos of new earrings, make a necklace-styling video, share the website link, post festival collection updates and invite satisfied customers to leave honest reviews.

Activity

Choose two digital marketing methods for your business and explain how you would use them.

Chapter 6: Social Media Marketing for Beginners

Social media helps small businesses introduce products and build relationships with customers.

How to get started

  1. Choose a platform that suits your customers and products.
  2. Create a business profile with your name, logo, description and contact information.
  3. Post regularly with product photos, demonstrations, new arrivals and useful tips.
  4. Write clear captions with product information, price and ordering details.
  5. Respond to customers politely and promptly.
  6. Track views, reach, engagement, enquiries and sales where those metrics are available.
Sample social media post

✨ Add elegance to your everyday look! ✨ Discover our beautiful collection of earrings for casual outings and special occasions.
💰 Price: ₹499
📩 For product details and orders, contact us through our business page.
#Jewellery #SmallBusiness #IndianJewellery #ShopLocal
Replace the sample price and product details with your actual information.

Important tips

  • Do not buy fake followers or fake reviews.
  • Avoid posting misleading offers.
  • Get permission before sharing customer photographs.
  • Do not send repeated promotional messages to people who have not agreed to receive them.

Chapter 7: Understanding Customers and Building Trust

A successful digital business does more than display products. It also helps customers feel confident about buying.

Who is your target audience?

A target audience is the group of people most likely to buy your products or services. For a jewellery business, this could include people seeking everyday accessories, festive jewellery, wedding gifts or affordable fashion items.

How to understand customers

  • Ask customers what products they prefer and what price ranges suit them.
  • Identify common questions and concerns.
  • Review genuine customer feedback.
  • Observe which products receive more enquiries.

How to build trust online

  • Display accurate product information and genuine photographs.
  • Explain delivery charges and timelines clearly.
  • Provide a clear return or exchange policy, where applicable.
  • Share authentic customer reviews only with permission.
  • Respond professionally to complaints and protect customer contact and payment information.
Example

If a customer asks whether a necklace is real gold, explain its actual material instead of making a false claim.

Activity

Write three questions a customer might ask before buying and prepare clear answers.

Chapter 8: Getting Orders, Payments, and Deliveries Online

Receiving and confirming orders

Depending on the website, customers may order through a website form, WhatsApp or phone, a shopping cart and checkout, or a marketplace. Confirm the product name and quantity, final price, delivery charges, delivery address when required, estimated delivery date and payment method.

Understanding payment options

Businesses may accept cash on delivery, bank transfers, UPI or payments through a suitable payment gateway, depending on their setup. Never display sensitive customer payment information publicly. A sample website should use demonstration data, not real customer payment details.

Delivery and customer service

  • Pack products safely and share accurate delivery information.
  • Keep customers informed about delays.
  • Provide a way to contact the business for help.
  • Handle returns and complaints according to the stated policy and applicable rules.

Chapter 9: Basic Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

SEO helps a website communicate clearly what a business sells, which can improve its chances of appearing in relevant search results. Results are not guaranteed.

Important SEO terms

  • Keyword: A phrase customers may search for, such as “affordable jewellery in Palghar.”
  • Page title: The title that describes a webpage.
  • Meta description: A short summary that may appear in search results.
  • Image alt text: Text describing an image for accessibility and to help search engines understand it.
  • Internal link: A link from one page of your website to another.
  • Mobile-friendly website: A website that works well on smartphones.
Example

Instead of the vague title “Products,” use a clearer title such as “Fashion Earrings and Necklaces | Shine Jewellery.”

Basic SEO checklist

  • Give each important page a clear title.
  • Use relevant words naturally in product names and descriptions.
  • Add descriptive text for product images and make the website mobile-friendly.
  • Keep contact information accurate and avoid copying descriptions from other websites.
Activity

Write one page title and three relevant keywords for your business.

Chapter 10: Measuring Results and Improving Your Business

Digital marketing is not only about posting products. Business owners should also understand what is working and what needs improvement.

Important performance measures

  • Website visits: How many visits the website receives.
  • Enquiries: How many people ask about products or services.
  • Conversion rate: The percentage of visitors who complete a desired action, such as buying.
  • Engagement: Interactions such as comments, shares and saves.
  • Sales: The number or value of completed purchases.
  • Customer feedback: Opinions and suggestions that can help improve the business.
Example

If 100 people visit a product page and 5 people buy, the purchase conversion rate is 5%.
Conversion rate = (Purchases ÷ Visitors) × 100

How to improve results

  • Improve unclear product descriptions and use better photographs.
  • Make contact information easy to find and respond to enquiries quickly.
  • Review which posts generate genuine interest.
  • Ask customers for feedback and act on useful suggestions.
Activity

At the end of each month, record website visits, enquiries, orders, sales and customer suggestions, if the relevant data is available.
